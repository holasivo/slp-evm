## Announce

- **Migration to Chainlink SLP Oracle** — Live since 11 September, ahead of Thursday: the SLP hook on mainnet now prices every deposit and withdrawal from the Chainlink SLP/USD feed, which publishes the NAV calculated by Accountable on-chain, instead of Sivo's own operator-published oracle. The cutover was a single Safe transaction prepared by a script that checks the Safe holds the right role, that the feed is fresh and inside the configured band, and that the call succeeds before proposing it; the contract deployment defaults now point at the same feed. This is the contract side of the entry with the same name in `sivo-xyz/releases/2026-09-17.md` — one line under DeFi, not two. (f397c536; already in production)
